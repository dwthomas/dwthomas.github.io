I study heuristic search and situated planning.

I am a member of [the UNH Artificial Intelligence Group](https://unh-ai.pbworks.com/w/page/10440225/FrontPage), where I work with Professor [Wheeler Ruml](https://www.cs.unh.edu/~ruml/).

I began working at UNH in 2017, working in bioinformatics at [the Hubbard Center For Genome Studies](https://hcgs.unh.edu/), and [environmental DNA](https://www.estuarydna.org/) with Professor [Alison Watts](https://ceps.unh.edu/person/alison-watts).

I have a B.Sc. with combined honours in physics and astronomy from the University of British Columbia. My undergraduate thesis was on detecting point sources in data from the [CHIME](https://chime-experiment.ca) Pathfinder telescope, supervised by Professor [Gary Hinshaw](https://phas.ubc.ca/users/gary-hinshaw).
